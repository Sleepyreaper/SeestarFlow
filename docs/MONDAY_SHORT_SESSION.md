## App scheduling

Plan Mode schedules targets, imaging windows and filters, and can automatically slew between them. Target switching is optional: tracking already follows Earth's rotation. Scheduling lets us leave an obstructed or less favorable target and use another available window. It does not itself eliminate Alt-Az field rotation. Do not assume the app recognizes local trees, rooflines or lamp glare; these constraints must come from our survey and actual testing.

On arrival, confirm available exposure choices, individual-frame saving, per-target filter handling, scheduling controls, and whether live-stack previews are accessible during Plan Mode on the installed version. Use normal Stargazing/manual switching if that gives the clearest live demonstration. Set an explicit finish time rather than filling the whole night.
