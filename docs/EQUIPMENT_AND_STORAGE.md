# What is required now and what can wait

## Monday: buy nothing else

Use the supplied carbon tripod in Alt-Az, the already ordered Anker power bank,
red headlamp, included USB-C data cable, phone/tablet, and a weighted opaque lamp
shield. The TH10/quick-release plate is for the later EQ phase.

The processing computer currently reports about 90 GB free. That is ample for
Monday's short commissioning session but marginal for a full eight-hour 10s run
plus registration products and a second copy.

## Before the first multi-hour road session

Add two storage destinations:

1. a **2 TB rugged USB-C SSD** as the working capture/archive drive; and
2. a second independent 2 TB drive or an automatic off-site backup as the
   verified backup.

One drive with two folders is not two backups. Do not rely on the telescope or
laptop as the only copy. Keep the backup drive physically separate while
traveling.

A Samsung T7 Shield-class drive is a sensible reference design: Samsung lists
USB 3.2 Gen 2, up to roughly 1,050/1,000 MB/s, IP65 resistance, and included
USB-C-to-C/USB-C-to-A cables. The [official T7 Shield comparison](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10002047/)
supports those specifications. A Crucial X9 Pro-class drive is another compact
option; its [official product sheet](https://www.crucial.com/content/dam/crucial/ssd-products/x9-pro/flyer/crucial-X9-productflyer.pdf)
lists IP55 resistance and drop protection. Buy on value and warranty; this
workflow does not need their peak speed.

Neither IP rating means the drive should be left in dew or rain. Transfer in
a dry vehicle or indoors and keep caps/cables clean.

## Before Tromsø

- prove the power bank, cable, and dew heater together for at least three hours;
- keep batteries in carry-on baggage and follow the airline's current limits;
- pack the telescope in carry-on only if dimensions and airline policy allow;
- bring a condensation strategy: seal cold equipment before entering warm humid
  air and let it warm gradually;
- verify operation remains within the manual's -10°C to 40°C recommended range;
- expect reduced battery performance in cold conditions.

## Before Abaco

- use a stable ground-level patio position, never loose beach sand;
- shield from salt spray and wind; do not operate if spray can reach the optics
  or electronics;
- keep the scope capped/cased in air conditioning until a planned acclimation;
- handle condensation deliberately when moving between cool indoor air and warm
  humid outdoor air;
- prioritize short supervised sessions and copy data after every night.
